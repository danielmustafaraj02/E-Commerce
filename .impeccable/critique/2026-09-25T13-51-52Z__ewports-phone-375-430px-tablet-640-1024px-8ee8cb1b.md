## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: Mobile experience is genuinely composed, not desktop-squeezed-down, in the storefront's interactive surfaces (gift-finder one-question-per-screen, look composer's tap+swipe model instead of drag-and-drop, cart line grid restructuring at a bespoke 34rem breakpoint, purpose-built sticky mobile Add-to-Cart bar). Uneven though: the header (shared by every page) has the site's most severe mobile gap, and admin touch targets silently fall outside the site's own documented rule.

Deterministic scan: fresh full rescan, same 13 primary / 206 advisory findings as before (bounce-easing mostly verified-intentional; layout-transition x2 still unresolved, out of today's fix scope). Zero findings in all six files edited this session. Grep evidence: overflow-x-auto used in 16 admin files; only one file site-wide (globals.css) declares the pointer:coarse touch-target rule. A few fixed-rem widths found are expected decorative/icon sizing, not real overflow risks.

Visual overlays: unavailable, no browser tool or live server this session.

## Overall Impression

Where the team invested (look composer, quantity stepper, mobile nav menu) mobile craft is genuinely better than most sites in this category. But the header has a real gap: no way to sign in, view account, or open wishlist at all on a phone — a harder floor than any animation or copy problem, sitting directly upstream of PRODUCT.md's gift-buyer/self-purchaser audience.

## What's Working

1. Look composer's touch model (src/components/look-composer.tsx) - tap-based selection, native scroll-snap rows, custom progress track. Degrades to touch cleanly.
2. Cart quantity stepper (src/components/quantity-stepper.tsx) - explicit 44x44px buttons, aria-labels, aria-live count. Done exactly right.
3. Mobile nav menu's focus management (src/components/mobile-nav-menu.tsx) - real dialog semantics, scroll lock, managed focus, Escape-to-close.

## Priority Issues

[P0] Account, Wishlist, and Sign-in are unreachable at phone widths
- Why it matters: Below 640px the header's entire icon cluster is hidden sm:flex. mobileNavLinks in header.tsx has no Account/Wishlist/Sign-in entry. Footer checked too, zero matches. A returning customer on a phone has no discoverable path to log in or open their wishlist.
- Fix: Add Account/Sign-in (and Wishlist when logged in) to mobileNavLinks and the mobile menu's secondary row, or a persistent fourth header icon.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

[P1] Header category nav has no overflow handling - and the fix was already half-built and abandoned
- Why it matters: globals.css defines .scrollbar-hide/.edge-fade-x with a comment citing this exact problem, but they're used in zero .tsx files - dead CSS. The nav itself (active from 640px) has no wrap/scroll/truncation. In an 11-locale store this is a "when," not "if."
- Fix: Wire .scrollbar-hide/.edge-fade-x (or overflow-x-auto) onto the header's category nav.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

[P1] A 640-1279px band hides search and the heritage story simultaneously
- Why it matters: Search is hidden lg:flex (gone below 1024px); About/guide links are hidden xl:inline-flex (gone below 1280px); the mobile hamburger (which carries both) disappears at 640px. Virtually every tablet in portrait has no search and no heritage link anywhere in primary nav - against PRODUCT.md Principle #3.
- Fix: Re-tier breakpoints so at least one of {mobile menu, search, About/guide} is always present between 0-1024px.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt

[P2] Admin touch targets fall below the site's own documented rule at tablet width
- Why it matters: DESIGN.md commits to 2.75-2.9rem touch targets "site-wide" under pointer:coarse, but that rule (declared in exactly one place) only covers .btn-*/.field. Admin's status pills (~28px) and sidebar nav links (~36px) never inherit it. Admin is desktop-first by design but PRODUCT.md accepts staff may open it on tablet, and the rest of admin's tablet adaptation is otherwise solid - this is a specific, fixable miss.
- Fix: Extend the pointer:coarse rule to cover these pill/link patterns, or route them through the existing button-equivalent classes.
- Suggested command: /impeccable harden

Carryover, not re-scored here: the transition:width layout-thrashing issue on the free-shipping progress bar, flagged in the first critique, is still unfixed - it renders at every width including mobile.

But on a tablet with a motor impairment she hits the same sub-44px admin controls as Casey (P2). Checkout shipping-quote region is aria-live="polite" but has no interim loading announcement.
