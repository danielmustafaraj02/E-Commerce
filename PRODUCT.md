# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Gift buyers and people buying jewelry for themselves. Both need clear product photos, reliable delivery information and confidence in authenticity.

## Product Purpose

Perla Murano Glass sells genuine Murano glass jewelry at `perlamuranoglass.com`. Help customers choose a piece and complete a purchase, either for themselves or as a gift.

## Positioning

Lead with craftsmanship, Murano provenance and the glassmaking tradition. Use accurate product-specific claims.

## Operating Context

The storefront offers catalog browsing, looks, wishlist, gift finder, gift cards, journal and guest checkout in 11 languages. The private local admin manages products, orders, suppliers, content and design. Accounts and sign-in are local-only.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Next.js, Prisma/PostgreSQL, Stripe/PayPal and Resend. Products and reviews come from real records. Support Arabic right-to-left layout and non-Latin scripts as well as English and Italian.

## Brand Commitments

- Use **Perla Murano Glass** consistently.
- Claim the protected **Vetro Artistico® Murano** certification only for products that actually hold it.
- Do not invent products, reviews, press mentions or performance results.

## Voice

One person who knows glass, writing plainly. Let the objects show their value; don't announce it.

- Prefer what can be seen or checked (a colour, a clasp, a length, a date with a source) over adjectives. Cut any sentence that adds no fact, feeling or clarity.
- Never invent techniques, makers, provenance, certifications or customer behaviour. Not every piece is made by us or made the same way; say "Murano glass", not "handmade on Murano", unless the product record says so. History claims follow the sourced journal articles (for example: glass was worked on Murano before 1291; the 1291 decree closed furnaces in the city).
- Avoid stock luxury and AI phrasing: "timeless elegance", "exquisite craftsmanship", "more than just jewelry", "each piece tells a story", "where tradition meets innovation", "unique", "magia di Venezia", "lasciati conquistare". No exclamation marks, no false urgency.
- Short sentences, with the occasional longer one. Write sentences, not comma chains: join clauses with a full stop, colon or semicolon.
- English and Italian are each written by a native ear, not translated line by line. Italian uses sentence case ("Collane in vetro di Murano"), "tu" in the shop, and no constructions that only make sense translated back into English.
- Search terms (Murano glass jewelry/necklaces/bracelets/earrings; gioielli/collane/bracciali/orecchini in vetro di Murano) go where a person would naturally use them: titles, the first paragraph, headings. Never repeat them for their own sake.
- Microcopy stays plain: buttons, shipping, prices and checkout say exactly what happens.

## Product Principles

Show the jewelry clearly, make gifting easy, explain costs and delivery before payment, and keep the craftsmanship story readable. Store operations need the same clarity as the storefront.
