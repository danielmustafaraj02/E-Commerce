# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Gift buyers and people buying jewelry for themselves. Both need clear product photos, reliable delivery information and confidence in authenticity.

## Product Purpose

Perla Murano Glass sells genuine Murano glass jewelry at `perlamuranoglass.com`. Help customers choose a piece and complete a purchase, either for themselves or as a gift.

## Positioning

Lead with craftsmanship, Murano provenance and the glassmaking tradition. Use accurate product-specific claims.

## Operating Context

The storefront offers catalog browsing, looks, wishlist, gift finder, gift cards, journal and guest checkout in 11 languages. The private local admin manages products, orders, suppliers, content and design. Accounts and sign-in are local-only.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Next.js, Prisma/PostgreSQL, Stripe/PayPal and Resend. Products and reviews come from real records. Support Arabic right-to-left layout and non-Latin scripts as well as English and Italian.

## Brand Commitments

- Use **Perla Murano Glass** consistently.
- Claim the protected **Vetro Artistico® Murano** certification only for products that actually hold it.
- Do not invent products, reviews, press mentions or performance results.

## Product Principles

Show the jewelry clearly, make gifting easy, explain costs and delivery before payment, and keep the craftsmanship story readable. Store operations need the same clarity as the storefront.
